3. On-device storage, backups and cloud synchronisation

Information kept on your Android device

The app uses a local Room database and app-private preferences to support offline operation. Your ledger may therefore remain on your phone even when there is no internet connection. Reminder generation and several app preferences are processed locally using the records and settings on your device.

Local app data is stored in the application's private Android storage. The app's PIN and biometric options are access controls; they should not be treated as a guarantee that every database file is separately encrypted. Keep your phone protected with a device screen lock and do not share your credentials.

Cloud backup and Pro synchronisation

When you sign in and use cloud backup or synchronisation, selected profile and ledger information is sent to Google Cloud Firestore under your account. The free tier uses a compact backup document and is subject to current limits, including up to 100 debtors and 150 inventory entries. The Pro tier uses separate cloud records for live publishing and synchronisation. Private companion records are restricted to the business owner; the customer portal only exposes records that meet the app's customer-safe rules and have an active authorised connection.

Pro cloud publishing is designed around one active publishing phone for a business account at a time. Moving publishing to another phone may require an ownership transfer and cloud restoration. If a subscription expires after migration, some cloud writes and paid features may be disabled. Expiry alone does not automatically delete your local ledger or existing cloud ledger.

Automatic backup is conditional: the app checks for changed data and network availability and may perform a backup when due. A successful backup is not guaranteed merely because the feature is enabled. Check the app's backup status and keep an independent copy of important financial records.

Important: Signing out is not the same as deleting an account or deleting cloud data. Use the account-deletion process in section 9 when you want your account data removed.

5. Document scanning, OCR and AI

Debt Ledger offers two document-reading paths. The document-scanner and text-recognition flow uses Google Play services document-scanning components and ML Kit text recognition. The separate AI scan flow resizes and compresses the image you submit, then sends it over an authenticated HTTPS request to our backend. The backend forwards the image to Google Cloud Vertex AI using a Gemini model configured for the service and receives structured suggestions such as debtor names, item names, quantities and prices.

The backend's scan-request records contain request identifiers/fingerprints and the structured result; the reviewed scan function does not write the original image into those Firestore scan-request records. The structured result and attempt counters are configured with a seven-day expiry. The image is nevertheless transmitted to Google Cloud for the requested AI processing, and Google's service terms and safety/abuse controls may govern processing outside our own Firestore records.

Google Cloud states that it does not use customer data to train or fine-tune Google AI/ML models without prior permission or instruction. That statement is subject to the Google Cloud terms and service configuration in force at the time; it is not a promise that no temporary processing, security review or abuse monitoring can ever occur. Review Google's current AI Trust and Transparency information and Service Specific Terms.

AI and OCR results can be incomplete or wrong. The app presents the extracted information for you to review and edit before saving it as a financial record. Do not rely on an unverified scan as proof of a debt, payment, identity or legal obligation. Only submit images you have the right to use, and avoid including unnecessary identity numbers, health details or other highly sensitive information.

7. When information is shared

We do not sell your debtor/customer ledger or customer list. Information may be disclosed in the following limited situations:

  • Service providers: Google and its relevant service components process information to provide Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check, Vertex AI/Gemini, Google Play Billing and Google Mobile Ads/UMP. These providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy documents.
  • Customer portal: If you use an available customer-portal feature, a business may invite a customer to connect to a specific debtor record. After the customer redeems an invitation and the connection is active, the customer may see only the customer-safe debtor, debt and payment records allowed by the service. The business can revoke the connection, and the customer can disconnect. Business Pro access is required for the portal under the current backend configuration. Invitation codes are single-use, expire after 30 minutes and are stored as hashes by the backend.
  • Your own sharing: If you export a report/receipt or choose to share content through Android, email, WhatsApp or another application, the recipient and that service may receive the content you selected. Their own privacy terms then apply.
  • Legal and safety reasons: We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect users or the public, investigate abuse, or enforce our terms.

Google Play handles the payment instrument and checkout for subscriptions and scan packs. The app/backend receive purchase-verification information such as product identifiers, purchase tokens and order references, but the app does not collect your full card number or online-banking password.

9. Retention, account closure and deletion

We keep information for as long as it is needed to provide the service, maintain your account and ledger, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes, protect the service and prevent purchase or credit abuse. Different information has different retention periods.

  • Local records: Records stored on your phone remain in the app's local storage until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall the app, subject to Android/device behaviour and any copies you export or share.
  • Cloud ledger and profile: Cloud records are retained while the relevant account/feature remains active or until a valid deletion request is completed, except for records that must be retained for the purposes described below.
  • AI scan records: Successful scan results and scan-attempt counters have a configured seven-day expiry. Expiry cleanup is automatic and may not occur at the exact moment the period ends.
  • Billing verification: Raw purchase tokens may be held in server-only retry/refresh tasks and are configured to expire 30 days after the verified subscription expiry, or be removed during role deletion. Hashed purchase identifiers, order references, grant/refund markers, account-generation tombstones and the account-linked scan-credit balance are currently retained indefinitely to prevent duplicate credits and reconcile purchase/refund history. These retained records mean deletion is not the same as erasing every identifier or billing-related record.
  • Invitation data: Invitation codes are stored as hashes and are valid for 30 minutes; expired or used invitations are no longer usable.

Request deletion of your Debt Ledger account

In the app: open More → Delete Business Account, confirm the request and complete any recent sign-in verification requested by the app.

Without the app: email khadharahmed91@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Use the subject “Debt Ledger account deletion request” and state whether you are requesting deletion of a business account or a customer/portal account. Do not send your password or a purchase token.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you control the account. A deletion request disables the relevant role and starts a resumable server-side cleanup process; the request may show as pending while the cleanup runs. The process is not necessarily instantaneous. Business/customer ledger data, private companion records, invitations, customer connections, scan results, active billing tasks and relevant role profiles are scheduled for deletion. If no active role remains, the Firebase Authentication account is also deleted.

Limited retention: the pseudonymous billing and anti-replay records listed above remain under the current backend design. We will not describe the account as fully erased while those records remain. If a law requires deletion of additional information or prohibits a particular retention, contact us so the request can be assessed under that law.

Deleting a Debt Ledger account does not cancel a recurring Google Play subscription. Cancel the subscription separately in Google Play to prevent future renewal charges. Deleting a record inside the ledger is also different from deleting the entire account.
